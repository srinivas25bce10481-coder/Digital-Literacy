For this task, I designed a one-page infographic titled “Digital Skills for Students” using Canva. The aim of this visual was to present key concepts of digital literacy in a clear, structured, and visually engaging format that can be easily understood by fellow students.

The infographic is organized into multiple color-coded sections, each focusing on an essential aspect of digital literacy. It begins with a concise definition to establish a foundational understanding. The subsequent sections highlight useful digital tools such as cloud storage and online learning platforms, followed by safe internet practices with emphasis on privacy, strong passwords, and responsible browsing. Another section focuses on building a professional online presence, encouraging students to maintain a positive digital footprint and engage in professional networking. Additionally, a dedicated segment provides quick email etiquette tips, including the use of clear subject lines and proofreading before sending messages.

While creating this infographic, one of the main challenges I encountered was effectively organizing a wide range of information within limited space without making the design appear cluttered. Transforming complex ideas into short, impactful bullet points required careful thought and creativity. Overall, this task enhanced my ability to communicate important information visually and strengthened my understanding of digital literacy concepts.
