Case Study: UPI Payment Fraud (The "Cashback" Scam)

What it is: UPI scams happen when fraudsters trick people into approving bank transfers through UPI apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) by pretending to offer cashback, refunds, or lucky draw prizes.

How it happens (Step-by-Step):

First Contact: Scammer sends WhatsApp message or calls pretending to be from Flipkart, Amazon, or bank support, saying you have pending refund money.

Gaining Trust: They sound professional and mention your name or phone number (easily available online) to seem genuine.

The Bait: They send payment request link, QR code, or UPI notification to your phone.

The Trick: Tell you to "check your cashback" by opening UPI app → tap their link → enter UPI PIN → press "Pay".

The Theft: UPI PIN sends money OUT. Your account money goes straight to scammer instantly.

Who gets targeted: College students selling gadgets on OLX, elderly getting "scheme money," anyone clicking unknown links.

Consequences: Money gone forever (UPI transfers can't be reversed), plus stress, police visits, and fear of online payments.
