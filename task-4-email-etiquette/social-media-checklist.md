The Do's (Smart Usage):

1.Regularly check and update privacy settings on all your social platforms.

2.Highlight your academic projects, coding work, internships, and achievements.

3.Google yourself monthly to monitor your online presence for recruiters.

4.Think twice before posting - especially when upset or in a heated moment.

5.Use LinkedIn, GitHub, and Twitter to connect with AI/ML professionals and peers.

The Don'ts (Avoid These Traps):

1.Never post rants or negative comments about professors, college, or classmates.

2.Keep personal details private (address, timetable, contact info, family details).

3.Stay out of online fights, trolling, or controversial debates - screenshots last forever.

4.Avoid photos showing inappropriate behavior, underage drinking, or unprofessional conduct.

Remember: nothing online is ever truly deleted - employers Google everything.
